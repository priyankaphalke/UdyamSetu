## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, dependable, and precision-driven digital workspace tailored for public-sector enterprise compliance, administrative workflows, and state regulatory orchestration in Maharashtra. The target audience comprises industrial compliance directors, state bureau administrators, and MSME/enterprise operators navigating multi-departmental clearances.

The aesthetic philosophy is rooted in **Modern Corporate Institutionalism**:
- Uncompromising clarity over decoration. 
- Restrained visual density that commands bureaucratic and statutory legitimacy.
- Clear structural division using fine hair-line architectural dividers, crisp tabular layouts, and clear status ledgers.
- Absolute rejection of decorative trends: no ambient blur effects, no decorative 3D assets, no illustrative AI tropes, and zero color gradients. Every border, fill, and text block represents real-world statutory accountability.

## Colors

The color palette is deliberately calibrated for high visual rigor, structural authority, and clear administrative distinction:

- **Primary (`#12304A` - Deep Navy):** Represents state sovereignty, institutional gravity, and authoritative anchoring. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level headers, active navigation rails, and primary structural accents.
- **Secondary (`#176B55` - Forest Green):** Denotes statutory clearance, verified compliance status, institutional trust, and positive operational milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#D99A35` - Muted Amber):** Dedicated to procedural prerequisites, regulatory pendency, audit warnings, and mandatory user-action triggers.
- **Neutral Core (`#263238` - Dark Slate):** Applied to primary body typography, structured tabular borders, and strict diagrammatic flows for high legibility without pure black harshness.
- **Surface Neutrals:**
  - Page Background: `#F7F8F6` (Warm institutional off-white minimizing eye strain across lengthy audit workflows).
  - Canvas/Card Fill: `#FFFFFF` (Stark clean slate for compliance documentation).
  - Subordinate Borders: `#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1` (Crisp 1px delimiters distinguishing data rows and modular sections).

## Typography

The typography uses Inter across all levels to maintain systematic neutrality, optical clarity, and robust tabular alignment. 

- Headings focus on structured weight distribution (600 to 700) with controlled, negative tracking to maintain compactness on complex multi-tier dashboards.
- Numeric and status-critical data fields leverage fixed-metric alignment and standard tabular figures to ensure numbers align neatly across columns in financial disclosures, tariff charts, and compliance trackers.
- Small labels and statutory codes utilize slight positive letter spacing and medium/semibold weights to ensure complete legibility on low-dpi display devices in field or district administrative offices.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive layout built upon an 8-point baseline grid with a 4-point sub-grid for dense administrative panels.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a fixed or max-width container with 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Content modules follow consistent horizontal division lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Multi-column forms reflow into single or dual paired sections.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) margins. Actionable tables adapt to structured vertical attribute lists or retain horizontal scroll with anchored statutory primary keys.
- **Layout Model:** High vertical precision. Component internals use `space-md` (16px) for compact modules and `space-lg` (24px) for expansive form step wrappers. Visual rhythm is sustained strictly through systematic padding and structural 1px lines rather than blank expansive whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through flat surface segmentation, strict border layering, and zero-diffusion containment:

- **Borders over Shadows:** Depth is produced by precise 1px solid borders (`#CBD5E1` for external boundaries, `#E2E8F0` for interior subdivisions) instead of drop shadows.
- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas rests at `#F7F8F6`. Cards, panels, and actionable data tables sit elevated on `#FFFFFF`. Sub-sections, table header rows, and disabled zones utilize neutral tints (`#F1F5F9` or `#F8FAFC`).
- **Overlays and Popovers:** When persistent context demands a floating layer (such as dropdown filters or action flyouts), an elevation shadow of `0 4px 12px rgba(18, 48, 74, 0.08)` coupled with a continuous 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`) is permitted to separate the active layer cleanly from the base sheet.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, restrained corner rounding scale (`roundedness: 1`):

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, badges, select pills) carry a radius of `4px` to `6px` (`rounded-md`).
- Container panels, statutory modal dialogs, and large data tables maintain a firm, architectural radius of `6px` to `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Elements must never use pill forms (`9999px`) or ultra-soft rounded corners, which run counter to statutory sobriety and enterprise density.
